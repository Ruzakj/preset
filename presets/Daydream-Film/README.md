# Daydream Film

Warm, airy daylight film preset family reconstructed from the supplied reference collage.

## Reference characteristics
- Natural daylight / golden backlight
- Warm-neutral to golden white balance
- Airy highlights, soft contrast, lifted film response
- Creamy whites, honey yellows, muted olive greens, restrained cyan/blue
- Fine analog grain and reduced digital microcontrast

## Presets
20 variants: Original, Clean, Soft, Golden, Cream, Portrait, Skin Safe, Field, Pastel, Bright, Backlight, Muted, Analog, Matte, Wedding, Travel, Earthy, Fade, Vivid, Signature.

## Recommended for
Outdoor portrait, family, wedding, lifestyle, golden hour, countryside, travel and soft daylight.

Exposure dan White Balance mungkin tetap perlu disesuaikan berdasarkan kondisi foto.

No crop, geometry, camera-specific lens correction, masking, or fixed exposure is embedded.
