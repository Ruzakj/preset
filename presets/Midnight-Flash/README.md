# Midnight Flash

A Lightroom preset family reconstructed from a low-light direct-flash reference with a mirror reflection.

## Reference characteristics

- **Lighting:** night / low-light interior with strong direct on-camera flash
- **White-balance impression:** warm-neutral skin and brown/amber ambient cast
- **Tonal style:** very deep ambient blacks, bright flash subject, compressed/soft highlight roll-off
- **Dominant colors:** warm skin/orange-brown midtones, near-black brown shadows, subdued greens/blues
- **Contrast:** strong scene contrast, but with a slightly soft/consumer-film response
- **Saturation:** moderately muted; skin remains warm and readable
- **Texture:** compact-camera / disposable-camera character with restrained clarity and visible grain
- **Intended photography:** candid flash, nightlife, street, indoor portrait, mirror shots, travel snapshots

## Color DNA

The family keeps warm skin, dark brown-black ambient shadows, muted cool colors, a gentle blue-channel highlight compression, and film-like grain. Exposure is intentionally not embedded so the presets remain reusable across cameras and scenes.

## Presets

01. **Original** — Closest reconstruction: warm direct-flash, deep brown-black shadows, muted cool colors.
02. **Clean** — Cleaner flash rendition with less fade/grain.
03. **Soft** — Softer contrast and gentler flash roll-off.
04. **Strong** — Punchier version of the reference DNA.
05. **Warm** — Warmer amber/brown interpretation.
06. **Cool** — Slightly cooler flash while retaining warm skin/shadows.
07. **Moody** — Deeper brown shadows for dark interiors.
08. **Bright** — Lighter tonal adaptation without embedding exposure.
09. **Dark** — Protects flash highlights while deepening ambient darkness.
10. **Portrait** — Skin-safe orange/red treatment and softer microcontrast.
11. **Street** — Harder compact-camera flash for candid street work.
12. **Film** — More analog curve, grain, muted chroma.
13. **Matte** — Lifted black floor with subdued flash contrast.
14. **Cinematic** — Stronger warm/cool separation while staying within reference palette.
15. **Night** — Low-light adaptation: controlled highlights, open enough shadows, stronger grain.
16. **Analog** — Disposable-camera interpretation with stronger grain and faded highlights.
17. **Signature** — Balanced final signature variant for general use.

## Recommended For

Candid flash photography, nightlife, indoor portraits, street photography, mirror shots, compact-camera aesthetics, travel snapshots, and low-light social photography.

## Usage

Exposure and White Balance may still need to be adjusted based on the photo and lighting conditions. For portraits, start with **Portrait** or **Clean**. For the closest reference match, start with **Original**. For very dark scenes, try **Night** or **Dark**.

No crop, geometry, camera-specific lens correction, subject mask, or fixed exposure is included.
